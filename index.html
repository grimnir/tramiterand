Cloudflare LavaRand™

A wall of ~100 lava lamps in an office lobby. A camera photographs the wax; pixel noise becomes a TLS seed. Beautiful, expensive, very Instagram. One problem: wax obeys thermodynamics. It heats up, it rises. It cools down, it sinks. That, sorry, can be modeled.

our method

TrámiteRand™ (Migraciones Edition)

Two identical applications, same window, four minutes apart: the first one ready in 72 hours, the second one vanished for 14 months and came back with someone else’s photo. No model in the universe reproduces this process, because it depends on nothing. Absolutely nothing.
